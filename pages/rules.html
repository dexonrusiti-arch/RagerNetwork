Privacy Policy

Thank you for choosing to play on the Rager Network Minecraft server. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information while you engage with our server. By accessing or using the server, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

▷ We may collect certain personal information, such as your username, IP address, and in-game activities, to enhance gameplay and balance the server's meta.

▷ We may also use third-party services and plugins on the server, which may collect data to understand user interests, preferences, and overall player experience.

2. Data Usage

▷ We use personal information collected from players to provide, maintain, and improve our services.

▷ Your personal information may be used to enhance gameplay, customize in-game experiences, and optimize server performance.

▷ We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring its confidentiality.

3. Sharing of Information

▷ We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties without your consent.

▷ We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the server, as long as they agree to keep the information confidential.

4. Security

▷ We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

▷ However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Private Anti-Cheat

▷ Our server utilizes a private anti-cheat system to maintain fair gameplay and ensure a positive gaming environment.

▷ The anti-cheat system may collect data related to gameplay, including but not limited to player movements, interactions, and usage of certain game features.

The collected data is securely stored on our server and is used solely for the purpose of detecting and preventing cheating or unauthorized activities.

6. Staff Member Actions

▷ In certain cases, a staff member may request a player to:

• Not leave the server until instructed to do so.

• Join a designated Discord room with a staff member for further investigation, if possible.

• Share their screen with a staff member to verify the absence of external software or cheats.

• Install and run applications necessary for the staff member.

▷ The applications used by staff members may include:

• Anydesk or Teamviewer

• LastActivity View

• USBReview

During the interaction with the player, the staff member will not collect any personal data. They will only run the applications and search for applications similar to CheatEngine or search within the folder where Minecraft is located.

▷ As a user of Rager Network, you acknowledge and agree that all purchases made on the server are final and non-refundable. We do not provide refunds for any virtual goods, in-game currency, or other items acquired through transactions, regardless of the reason.

8. Changes to this Policy

▷ We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. The most recent version will be posted on our server. Your continued use of our services following any changes indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

Minecraft / Discord Rules

Below are the rules that apply to both Minecraft server and Discord community. Please read carefully.

Minecraft Rules

▷ INSULTS: Players are allowed occasional profanity, but they must not insult other players.

▷ ADVERTISING: Any form of advertising is prohibited unless authorized by the staff team.

▷ HACKING: Hacking, cheating, or using third-party software in any form is prohibited.

▷ ASKING STAFF FOR ITEMS/RANK: Soliciting items or ranks from staff members is not allowed.

▷ SPAMMING: Spamming will get you muted from Rager Anticheat.

Discord Rules

▷ Be respectful: You must respect all users, regardless of your liking towards them. Treat others the way you want to be treated.

▷ No Inappropriate Language: The use of profanity should be kept to a minimum. Any derogatory language towards any user is prohibited.

▷ No spamming: Don't send a lot of small messages right after each other. Do not disrupt chat by spamming.

▷ No pornographic/adult/NSFW material: This is a community discord server for Minecraft server and not meant to share this kind of material.

▷ No advertisements: We do not tolerate any kind of advertisements, whether it be for other communities or streams. You can post your content if it is relevant and provides actual value (Video/Art).

▷ No offensive names and profile pictures: You will be asked to change your name or picture if the staff deems them inappropriate.

▷ Direct & Indirect Threats: Threats to other users of DDoS, Death, DoX, abuse, and other malicious threats are absolutely prohibited.

▷ Every channel has its own purpose: Do not chat in channels like bot-commands or suggestion channels.

Penalties

▷ Insulting others = mute up to 7 days (depends on severity)

▷ Advertising = 1. mute up to 7 days (depends on severity) / 2. Ban up to 7 days (depends on severity)

▷ Hacking:

- x ray or any visual cheat = Ban to 30 days

- move hacks (speed, flight etc.) = seasonal ban*

- combat hacks (hitbox, reach etc.) = seasonal ban*

- glitching hacks = ban up to 30 days

* A seasonal ban is a ban that lasts for an entire season on Rager Network

2 seasonal bans = permanent ban
