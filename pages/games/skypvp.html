Overview

SkyPvP is a Minecraft game mode where players explore floating islands, gather resources, fight enemies, and battle other players in a world built around survival and progression.

At Rager Network, we've expanded this classic concept with RPG elements, combining PvP, PvE, exploration, quests, and equipment progression into a single experience where players can forge their own path, whether through combat, adventure, or simply exploring the world.

Explore the World

Discover a vast world filled with unique locations, dungeons, hidden secrets, challenging areas, and valuable resources waiting to be found.

From peaceful exploration to dangerous territories, every corner offers new opportunities for adventure and progression.

PvP & PvE

Take on the world through PvP and PvE, with PvP available throughout the global map, allowing you to encounter and battle other players as you explore.

PvE can be found both across the global map and within dedicated dungeons, where you can challenge powerful enemies and take on increasingly dangerous encounters as you progress.

Events

Rager Network's world is constantly brought to life through a variety of PvP and PvE events, ranging from contested territories and airdrops to powerful bosses, invading armies, and mysterious encounters.

Take part in server-wide events such as King of the Hill, Airdrops, Golden Piggy, Aegis's Blessing, Grim Awakening, Necrotic Uprising, and Skirmish to earn valuable rewards and prove yourself against other players and the world's most dangerous threats.

For those looking for direct competition, staff-hosted 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 tournaments offer a more competitive experience, with rewards ranging from valuable in-game items to cash prizes during our Grand Tournaments.

King of the Hill

Fight other players to capture a designated territory on the map and claim valuable rewards for holding the hill.

Airdrops

An airdrop falls onto a random location on the map, giving the fastest player to reach it the chance to claim its valuable rewards.

Airdrop example, Armory airdrop.

Golden Piggy

A Golden Piggy and its guards randomly appear across the map, rewarding the players who manage to defeat the Piggy with valuable loot.

Aegis's Blessing

Aegis wanders through the Rager Realm, spreading his blessing and summoning souls that players can collect in exchange for rewards.

Worldbreaker

A powerful boss emerges on the global map with an enormous amount of health, requiring players to work together to defeat it and earn server-wide rewards and buffs.

Grim Awakening

The Grim Reaper summons an army of undead to his island, with each defeated soldier having a chance to drop valuable rewards.

Necrotic Uprising

A corrupted undead army invades designated locations across the map, bringing tougher enemies and significantly greater rewards for those brave enough to face them.

Skirmish

Blaze and Ender forces clash across their respective islands, with players able to infiltrate enemy territory and fight powerful soldiers for valuable loot.

1v1 Tournament

Compete head-to-head against another player in staff-hosted 1v1 tournaments for valuable rewards and prizes.

2v2 Tournament

Team up with a partner and battle another duo in staff-hosted 2v2 tournaments for rewards and prizes.

3v3 Tournament

Form a team of three and compete against other squads in staff-hosted 3v3 tournaments, with rewards ranging from valuable in-game items to cash prizes during Grand Tournaments.

Leaderboards

Compete for the top spots across multiple leaderboards, including ELO, KDR, Top Kills, and Top Streak, with ELO dynamically reflecting your combat performance and the strength of your opponents.

Reach the top of the rankings to earn valuable in-game rewards, exclusive ranks, and cash prizes, while the members of the highest-ranked clan can also claim exclusive rewards for their collective performance.

SkyPvP features six different crates, each offering its own selection of rewards and providing players with different ways to obtain valuable equipment, items, and other rewards.

Dungeons

Inferno Spire

A towering fortress consumed by flame, where warriors and guardians sworn to the infernal forces defend the spire from anyone foolish enough to enter. The deeper you venture, the more relentless its defenders become.

Mobs: Flame Warrior, Infernal Guardian, Blazing Sentinel

Faction: Blaze

Difficulty: ★

Ender Abyss

A dark realm shaped by the mysterious forces of the End, where twisted creatures patrol the depths and defend its secrets. Those who enter must face an increasingly dangerous collection of Ender-born enemies.

Mobs: Void Stalker, Shadow Guard, Ender Ripper, End Scuttler, Ender Witch

Faction: Ender

Difficulty: ★

Deserted Bazaar

Once a thriving marketplace, this abandoned bazaar has long since been overtaken by creatures lurking among its forgotten streets. Poisonous spiders and the terrifying Spider Queen now rule the ruins, while the Sand Wrath haunts the surrounding sands.

Mobs: Venom Spider, Spider Queen, Sand Wrath

Faction: Human

Difficulty: ★

Primal Grounds

A wild and ancient territory inhabited by a tribal civilization that has remained untouched by the outside world. Its warriors, shamans, and wardens fiercely protect their lands from those who dare trespass.

Mobs: Tribal Minion, Tribal Warden, Tribal Shaman

Faction: Human

Difficulty: ★★

Stronghold

A heavily fortified military stronghold defended by an organized legion prepared for war. Archers, brutes, beasts, and powerful commanders stand between you and whatever lies beyond its gates.

Mobs: Legion Archer, Legion Brute, Legion Beast, Judicator, Legion Mage

Faction: Human

Difficulty: ★★★

Twilight Grove

A mysterious forest where nature itself seems to have turned against those who enter. Ancient guardians and corrupted woodland creatures protect the grove, with the Verdant Warden standing as its most powerful defender.

Mobs: Twigling, Stoneguard, Sapshooter, Rooted Guard, Verdant Warden

Faction: Reaper

Difficulty: ★★★

Necrotic Depths

Deep beneath the world lies a place consumed by corruption, where the dead have been twisted into relentless creatures. The further you descend, the more powerful and corrupted the undead become.

Mobs: Corrupted Wretch, Corrupted Ravager, Corrupted Brute

Faction: Reaper

Difficulty: ★★★★

Reaper’s Shade

A place touched by the presence of the Grim Reaper, whose influence has left the land shrouded in death and darkness. Those who enter must confront one of the Reaper’s most feared manifestations.

Mob: Wither

Faction: Reaper

Difficulty: ★★★★

Shadowed Reflection

A strange realm where the boundaries between reality and reflection begin to disappear. Within its depths, an unnatural reflection awaits those willing to confront something that should never have existed.

Mob: Reflection

Faction: None

Difficulty: ★★★

Exiled Beast

An isolated domain where a powerful creature has been cast away from the world. Whatever drove the beast into exile remains unclear, but its strength makes approaching it a dangerous undertaking.

Mob: Warden

Faction: Lost Nation

Difficulty: ★★★★

Reaper’s Lair

Deep within his domain lies the lair of the Grim Reaper himself, a being whose hunger for souls has been fed by centuries of war. Few have dared to enter, and even fewer have returned unchanged.

Mob: Reaper

Faction: Reaper

Difficulty: ★★★★★

Wizard’s Vault

Hidden away from the world lies a vault belonging to the mysterious wizard whose influence reaches far beyond the realm. Whatever knowledge and power lies within is protected by the very being who has shaped so much of Rager Network’s history.

Mob: Wizard

Faction: Human

Difficulty: ★★★★

Last Dance

A haunting place where the remains of a fallen survivor linger, trapped between the world of the living and the dead. Entering this forgotten ground means confronting the final remnants of someone who once fought to survive.

Mob: Fallen Survivor

Faction: Lost Nation

Difficulty: ★★★★★

Ethernal Guardian

A sacred domain tied to Aegis, the guardian angel revered throughout Rager Network. Empowered by the mysterious wizard and locked in an ancient struggle with the Grim Reaper, Aegis remains one of the realm’s most formidable beings.

Mob: Aegis

Faction: Lost Nation

Difficulty: ★★★★★★

Additional Features

Kits

You can preview all the kits at the spawn by right-clicking on NPCs marked with the "preview" hologram.

Preview NPC's

Currency

On Rager Network, we have a special currency: emeralds. Players can trade with various NPCs on the map using these emeralds, which they can acquire through sources like crates, the shop, airdrops, or PvP events.

Disposal

Do you have any unnecessary items? You can get rid of them using the Trash bin signs located at the spawn. Right-clicking the Trash bin opens it, and you can deposit all your unwanted items there.

Trash Bin

Note that once you leave items in the Trash bin and close the window, they are permanently lost, and there is no way to retrieve them. So be careful about which items you leave in the trash bin!

Showoff

You have cool items, maybe a whole inventory full? Lots of Elo points or an impressive KDR? You can show it to other players using the following commands:

[i] or [item] : Shows your holding item

[inv] or [inventory] : Shows your inventory

[ec] or [ender] : Shows your enderchest

[kd] : Shows your kill / death ratio

[elo] : Shows your Elo points

[bal] : Shows your balance

Trade

No, you don't have to drop items on the ground and risk getting scammed. There's an easier way to trade.

Just type /trade <playername>, then wait for the player to accept the request.

Next, you can add items for the trade, and the other player does the same. Once both sides are satisfied with the trade, press ready, and that's it – it's that simple!
