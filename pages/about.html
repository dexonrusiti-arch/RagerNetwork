About Us

Welcome to Rager Network, a realm where legends are born and adventures unfold. Founded in 2020, Rager Network emerged as a vision to create a Minecraft community unlike any other. Dexon, the visionary founder, embarked on a journey to bring forth a realm of boundless joy and exhilarating gameplay for Minecraft enthusiasts. With each passing day, we continue to write new chapters in our story. As we unveil upcoming mini-games, including KitPvP, Prison, and a captivating RPG world, we invite you to be a part of the next epoch in our journey.

Backstory

July 7, 2017 — The Beginning

Rager Network's story began as Dexon-Craft, originally a Factions server that transitioned to SkyPvP after its first year. These were the foundations of what would eventually become Rager Network.

August 12, 2020 — A New Beginning

De4an joined as a regular player and quickly became involved in the server's growth, bringing in players and sharing ideas. When Rager Network was established, he became its first staff member.

May 12, 2021 — Rapid Growth

On May 12, 2021, YouTuber Sharpness released a video promoting Rager Network, bringing a huge influx of players. As the community grew, Dexon and De4an expanded the staff team, welcoming Dworgen, Specijalac and Alekksa in June.

January 12, 2023 — The End of an Era

After years of development and countless memories, Rager Network was officially shut down on January 12, 2023.

By that point, Dexon had taken on increasing responsibilities outside of the server, balancing work, university, and other commitments. With less time and motivation available for development, continuing the project became increasingly difficult.

The decision to close Rager Network marked the end of an important chapter — but not the end of its story. The community, memories, and ideas that had been built over the years remained.

September 29, 2026 — The Rebirth

After more than three years, Rager Network rises again.

The return marks more than simply reopening the server. It is the beginning of a new era — built upon the experience, ideas, and lessons of everything that came before it.

With a renewed staff team, a new direction, and a fresh vision for the future, Rager Network enters its next chapter. fy_snow joins the team, while De4an's time as a staff member comes to an end, marking a natural evolution of the people behind the project.

The server itself is evolving as well. The familiar SkyPvP foundation is being reimagined through an RPG-inspired experience, introducing new systems, progression, and possibilities while preserving the spirit that defined Rager Network from the beginning.

What started as Dexon-Craft in 2017 has now returned under the Rager Network name — older, more experienced, and ready to begin again.

This is not a continuation of the past. It is the beginning of what comes next.
